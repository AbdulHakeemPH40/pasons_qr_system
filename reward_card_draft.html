Games Zone rewards - design draft 2

The win ticket

What the guest sees the moment a game is won. One silver ticket with two faces: a free dessert, or a discount between 5 and 10 percent. Draft 2 cuts the card like a real ticket: cropped corners, a dashed tear line with notches on both sides, and the code sitting above that line. This file is a copy for review. Tell me what to change, and I will apply the final design to the project only after you approve it.

One ticket, any prize

The same silver ticket carries every reward. Only the prize line changes, so a dessert and a 10 percent discount feel like the same win.

Move your pointer across the ticket to see it tilt. The light moves on its own.

The two faces

Same ticket, two prizes. The dessert face is warm and gold. The discount face is plain silver, because the number is the prize.

Full reward rules

Written from the live engine on 16 October 2026. Playing is always free. A prize is paid only after the work below, and only to a guest who has given a name and a mobile number, or signed in with Google.

Chess Master

Four levels. Win the level you are on to open the next one. You cannot skip.

  • Level 1 win: nothing. Level 2 opens.
  • Level 2 win: nothing. Level 3 opens.
  • Level 3 win: free dessert. Level 4 opens.
  • Level 4 win: 10% off the whole bill.

2048

The prize is the 2048 tile, not a high score. A big score that never made the tile pays nothing.

  • First time the tile is reached: free dessert.
  • Reaches 2, 3 and 4: nothing. They are stored, not paid.
  • Fifth reach, and every later reach: 10% off. Never another dessert.

Memory Match

Never a discount. A dessert only, after a run of real rounds.

  • Five qualifying rounds inside 7 days.
  • A round qualifies at 400 points or more.
  • A short or weak round does not count toward the five.

Flappy Burger

Never a discount. One scoring round is enough, if it is a real round.

  • 25 pipes or more: free dessert.
  • 24 pipes or fewer: nothing.
  • The round must last at least 25 seconds, with 15 taps.

What one guest can take in a month

The count is on the mobile number, not the phone and not the browser. Clearing cookies, or playing on another device with the same number, does not start a new allowance.

One free dessert Per mobile number, per calendar month. Chess, 2048, Memory Match and Flappy Burger all share this one dessert.

One 10% discount Separate from the dessert. A dessert does not use up the discount, and a discount does not use up the dessert.

A new month resets both Redeeming at the till does not. A voided coupon does not count. Each code is valid 14 days and can be used once.

Name + mobile

Play history is stored on the profile that holds the name and mobile. A guest who has not signed in can play, and the climb is kept, but nothing is paid until the name and number are on that profile. A round that is too short to be real play pays nothing.

What to tell me before this goes into the project

  • Corner cut. The 4 cropped corners: bigger bites, smaller bites, or straight bevel cuts instead?
  • Tear line. Dashes longer or shorter? Should the notches on the left and right be bigger or smaller?
  • Stub copy. Keep "How to use / Show this code to our staff before you pay / Valid 14 days", or reword it?
  • Shine. Keep the moving light, slow it down, or remove it?
  • Tilt. Keep the pointer tilt, or make the ticket still?
  • Colours. Keep the silver and gold, or should the silver go warmer?
  • The discount steps. Today every discount is 10%. The 5% and 7% faces on this page are not paid by any game yet.
